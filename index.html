Project 01 / Strategic Marketing

Mock Strategic Marketing Presentation for Buffalo Wild Wings

This project focused on Buffalo Wild Wings and explored how the brand uses marketing to create customer value and drive engagement, rather than relying solely on product promotion. The core objective was to analyze marketing as an experience-driven process, showing how Buffalo Wild Wings connects with customers through entertainment, community, and brand interaction.

Customer Personas

We developed three unique customer personas representing key segments of BWW’s audience. Each persona outlined:

  • Demographics: age, lifestyle, and income characteristics.
  • Core needs: primary motivations and expectations.
  • Value sought: desired experiences and benefits.
  • Engagement tactics: events, digital promotions, loyalty rewards, and social media.

Value Propositions

Each persona included a concise value proposition statement explaining how Buffalo Wild Wings fulfills customer needs and strengthens brand loyalty.

How BWW Creates Customer Value

Through a combination of atmosphere, service, community, and multi-channel engagement.

Engagement vs. Selling

The project emphasized emotional connection, ongoing interaction, and experiential value rather than basic transactions.

Key Takeaway: Strategic marketing at Buffalo Wild Wings turns casual dining into a relationship-based experience, blending sports, food, and fan culture to sustain customer loyalty.
Project 02 / Data Analytics & Marketing

Mock Strategic Marketing Presentation for Spotify

This project centered on Spotify and explored how the company uses data analytics to transform raw information into actionable marketing insights. The main learning objective was to understand the full data → insight → decision process, showing how marketers turn large volumes of data into meaningful strategies that improve customer retention and loyalty.

Business Question: “Why are Gen Z users switching music platforms and playlists more frequently than before?”

Part 1 — Hypotheses

Developed three evidence-based explanations for Gen Z switching behavior. Each hypothesis included a behavioral reason, supporting data indicators like usage or pricing trends, and business implications for Spotify.

Part 2 — Data Sources

Identified four or more relevant data sources, including internal listening metrics, playlist creation statistics, skip rates, social media trends, and competitor pricing data.

Part 3 — Recommended Marketing Action

Proposed a strategic marketing action targeting Gen Z users, including the objective, target segment, implementation idea, and expected outcome such as increased retention or in-app engagement.

Part 4 — Data vs. Insight

Explained the difference between data, the raw facts collected, and insight, the meaningful story drawn from analysis, using a Spotify-specific example.

Deliverable: A one-slide “Insight Memo” outlining the business question, hypotheses, data sources, marketing action, and final strategic insight.

Key Takeaway: The assignment emphasized how Spotify leverages user data and personalization algorithms to strengthen listener loyalty, reduce churn, and make smarter, insight-driven marketing decisions.
